Crew default

Every work day uses this crew unless the PM changes that day on the Daily tab (for example 9 men, or 10-hour days). IronClad sends 6 men × 8 hours unless the estimator changes it.

☔ Rain day: the job is charged a 2-hour show-up per man (e.g. 6 men × 2 h = 12 MH). Mark it on the Daily tab with “Rain day”; the PM can change the number of men who showed up.

Tracking format

The format can't be changed partway through a job. Starting over erases all marked work for this job (crew hours are kept) so the PM can pick the other format and re-enter progress.
